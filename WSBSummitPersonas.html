P1Persona 1: Elena Rodriguez

Student Persona 1

Elena Rodriguez

B.S. Healthcare Administration

Introductory Accounting Course
“I can follow the example while the video is playing, but when I try a practice problem by myself, I don’t know where to begin. Then I start the whole module over again.”
Age36
TermFirst term
OccupationPatient services supervisor

Background and Story

Elena works full-time at a medical clinic and is raising two school-age children with her spouse. She enrolled at WGU to prepare for an operations leadership role in healthcare. She moved quickly through her first courses, but accounting is the first subject that has made her question whether she can succeed in the program. She is comfortable asking questions at work, yet she feels embarrassed that the course is not “clicking” as quickly as she expected.

Goals and Needs

  • Understand how to begin and organize an accounting problem.
  • Connect concepts to realistic business examples.
  • Prepare for an assessment planned in nine days.
  • Rebuild confidence through visible progress.

Strengths to Build On

  • Consistent study time four evenings each week.
  • Strong attention to detail in her professional role.
  • Willing to practice when she knows what to practice.
  • Motivated by a clear career goal.

What Elena Is Doing Now

  • Rewatches complete course videos and rereads entire lessons after missing practice questions.
  • Highlights definitions but rarely works problems without looking at an example.
  • Can explain a concept during a lesson, but struggles to choose the correct process independently.
  • Has not talked through a practice problem with another person because she thinks she should be able to figure it out alone.

Pain Points

  • Application gap: Understands explanations in the moment but cannot transfer them to a new problem.
  • Inefficient study loop: Restarts broad content instead of identifying and practicing the specific step that broke down.
  • Confidence: Interprets slow progress as evidence that she is “not an accounting person.”
  • Limited feedback: Needs a low-pressure way to test her reasoning and learn from errors.

Technology Comfort

Moderate to strong. Elena navigates WGU systems comfortably and uses video calls at work. She prefers clear directions and tools she can immediately apply during her next study session.

Your task: Which Academic Services support or combination of supports would you recommend for Elena? Identify the specific resource, tool, or experience that best fits her needs.
P2Persona 2: Devin Carter

Student Persona 2

Devin Carter

B.S. Business Management

Introductory Accounting Course
“Accounting isn’t even the only problem. By the time everyone is asleep, I’m exhausted, so I move my study plan to tomorrow. Now I’m behind, and I don’t know how to catch up.”
Age31
TermSecond term
OccupationDistribution shift supervisor

Background and Story

Devin earned a promotion last year and enrolled at WGU because a bachelor’s degree would qualify him for regional leadership opportunities. He works a rotating schedule and helps care for his father, who has ongoing health needs. Devin completed his first term successfully, but this term his routine has unraveled. Accounting feels intimidating, and being behind in a second course has made every study session feel like a choice about what to neglect.

Goals and Needs

  • Create a realistic plan around a rotating work schedule.
  • Regain momentum without trying to fix everything at once.
  • Complete accounting within the current term.
  • Feel connected to people who understand the WGU experience.

Strengths to Build On

  • Successfully completed his first term.
  • Strong leadership and troubleshooting skills at work.
  • Persistent when a goal feels achievable.
  • Supportive family that wants him to finish.

What Devin Is Doing Now

  • Plans for long evening study blocks, then skips studying when he cannot complete the entire block.
  • Checks the course several times a week but often spends the available time deciding what to do first.
  • Moves missed study sessions to the next day, creating a growing and discouraging backlog.
  • Reads student community posts occasionally but has never participated because he assumes everyone else is progressing faster.

Pain Points

  • Overwhelm: Sees the entire term as one large problem and struggles to identify a manageable next step.
  • All-or-nothing planning: Treats a shortened study session as not worth doing.
  • Competing responsibilities: Work and caregiving demands change from week to week.
  • Isolation: Has not told anyone at WGU how far behind he feels and is beginning to disengage.
  • Confidence: Worries that this term proves his first-term success was luck.

Technology Comfort

Moderate. Devin uses scheduling and inventory systems at work and can navigate WGU tools. He is most likely to use a resource when the purpose is clear, the first step is simple, and he knows what to expect.

Your task: Which Academic Services support or combination of supports would you recommend for Devin? Identify the specific resource, tool, or experience that best fits his needs.
